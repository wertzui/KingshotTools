How is this calculated?
  • The event is simulated many times. Average is the mean over all runs. Worst and Best are the low and high luck percentiles (default 5% / 95%) of each individual reward, so 90% of runs land between them.
  • Every attack (Pumpkin Man or Wicked Witch) costs 20 candies unless it is free through Prankster Time. 10 hits defeat one Pumpkin Man.
  • Defeating a Pumpkin Man: 2 Treasure Points, the defeat reward, and a 20% chance for a Wicked Witch encounter with 3-15 attacks.
  • Prankster Time: the chance starts at 0.2% and rises by 0.2% with every paid attack. It resets after Prankster Time triggers. 3-11 attacks are then free. Free attacks do not raise the chance.
  • Wicked Witch's Treasure: rolled once per encounter with 10 or more attacks. The chance is 0.6% at 10,000+ Prankster Points and 1% at 20,000+. Each attack gives 2 Prankster Points. Candies from the treasure are only listed, not spent again.
  • The one-time chest at 300 Prankster Points (150 attacks) gives 2 Mythic General Hero Shards, 3 Forgehammers and a 3h General Speedup.
  • Each unused candy (less than 20 left at the end) becomes a Lv.1 Custom Secured Resource Chest.
  • Speedups of different sizes (5m, 1h, 3h) are added together and shown as a time span.
